# Two mechanism-focused revision guides

Publication date: 2026-10-07. Author attribution uses the site's existing author configuration.

## Scope and differentiation

- Cardiac cycle and valve pressure: pressure-gradient snapshots, closed-valve intervals, ventricular volume and a worked output calculation. Extends blood-and-circulation without repeating its route-led account.
- PCR amplification and copy number: primer orientation, ideal counts with an explicit starting definition, efficiency assumptions and limits of band evidence. Extends molecular-tools-and-dna-analysis without repeating its cut/copy/separate overview.

Reviewed neighbouring coverage: blood-and-circulation, human-respiration, molecular-tools-and-dna-analysis, dna-rna-replication-transcription-translation and enzymes-and-enzyme-action.

No laboratory recipe, diagnostic advice or textbook figure is reproduced.

## Originality and teaching value

Body-only token counts: cardiac 1106; PCR 1144. Case-normalised eight-word exact-phrase comparison against all other authored guide bodies found zero shared eight-word phrases. This checks textual overlap, not proof of source originality or scientific accuracy.

Each guide has a topic-specific comparison table, explicit incorrect-answer reasoning, a NCERT anchor, three contextual related-guide links and an incoming link from its parent overview. No artificial word-count padding was added.

Original AI-assisted conceptual illustrations were inspected before use. The PCR primer placement was corrected during visual review. Captions explain the simplified model and do not imply an anatomical or experimental measurement.
