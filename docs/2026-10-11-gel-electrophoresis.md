# Gel electrophoresis publication brief

Date: 2026-10-11. Target: MedQGo English NEET-UG Biology library.

Learning outcome: infer relative linear DNA fragment lengths from a gel while identifying what band count, alignment and intensity cannot establish.

NCERT anchor: Class 12 Biology, Chapter 9, Biotechnology: Principles and Processes, 2026-27 reprint, section 9.2.1 (separation of DNA fragments). https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lebo109.pdf . The official PDF opened and confirmed chapter/edition; subsequent retrieval was intermittent. Supplementary mechanism checks: NHGRI Electrophoresis glossary; Addgene Agarose Gel Electrophoresis introduction; Addgene Plasmids 101 restriction digest analysis. No source figure is reproduced.

Distinct relationships: charge determines direction; matrix and fragment length determine relative migration; a ladder calibrates length; one position can contain multiple same-length sequences; separation differs from amplification and identity testing.

Confusion checks: smaller vs larger (measure from wells); band vs molecule (many molecules contribute); same length vs same sequence (equal-length strings can differ); linear vs uncut plasmid (shape can change migration).

Closest published guides inspected: molecular-tools-and-dna-analysis (tool-to-output overview); pcr-amplification-and-copy-number (primer-cycle and molecule-count reasoning); recombinant-dna-technology (construct/host workflow). All three URLs returned 200 with article content before drafting.

Difference: this guide follows an evidence-reading path through one hypothetical gel, rather than repeating tool selection, amplification arithmetic or cloning order. The table tests conclusions against band observations. A novel 2400/1200/600 bp example and co-migration counterexample replace copying counts. Recall requires a sketched lane plus separate supported and unsupported claims. The original figure shows a length ladder and two aligned but unidentified sample bands; it does not use the existing PCR cycle or cloning process layout.

Prerequisite: molecular tools overview. Follow-on: PCR output interpretation and recombinant-DNA workflow. Three contextual links are planned to verified live authored guides. Exclusions: clinical interpretation, forensic identity claims, assay recipes and detailed gel calibration mathematics.

Publication checks will be recorded after integration and deployment.
