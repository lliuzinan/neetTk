## Difference from existing guides

The photosynthesis overview covers light reactions and the overall Calvin-cycle account. This guide instead follows an intercellular shuttle, distinguishes PEP/OAA/RuBP and uses an invented six-round carbon ledger. The anatomy guide locates tissue systems without this chemical account. The respiration guide concerns cellular oxidation and RQ, not Rubisco oxygenation. Heading order, comparison axis, aside and recall task were written for these distinctions.
