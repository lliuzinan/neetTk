# Ecosystem Energy Guide Publishing Note

Published: 2026-09-20

## Scope

This guide explains energy flow, primary productivity, trophic levels,
ecological pyramids and decomposition for the NEET-UG Biology ecosystem
chapter. It is an independent revision guide, not a reproduction of NCERT
text, figures or assessment material.

## Originality Audit

Closest existing guides:

- `organisms-and-populations`: population attributes, resource limits and
  growth models. The new guide instead follows energy and nutrients across
  trophic roles.
- `photosynthesis-in-higher-plants`: chloroplast processes and carbon
  fixation. The new guide starts after producer capture and traces ecosystem
  transfers.
- `plant-respiration`: cellular oxidation of glucose. The new guide explains
  why usable energy falls between trophic levels.

The learning outcome is distinct: a reader should be able to identify the
quantity represented by a pyramid and explain why energy flows while matter
cycles. Its central mechanism is producer fixation -> producer respiration ->
NPP -> trophic transfer -> decomposition and mineral return.

## Checks Included

- Comparison table distinguishes number, biomass, energy and productivity.
- Common confusions: energy versus nutrients; GPP versus NPP; standing biomass
  versus productivity; food chain versus food web.
- Recall routine asks the reader to draw a labelled energy path and a separate
  nutrient loop.
- The illustration is an original commissioned MedQGo teaching diagram. It is
  not derived from an NCERT or third-party figure.
- References point to the relevant NCERT chapter and a supporting OpenStax
  explanation.
