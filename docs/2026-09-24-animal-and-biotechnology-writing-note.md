# Animal and biotechnology guides: September 24, 2026

## Guides prepared

- Animal Kingdom: classification through independent evidence: organisation, symmetry, germ layers, body cavity and chordate features.
- Structural Organisation in Animals: a composition-led route from cells to tissues, organs and systems; compares the four primary tissue categories.
- Biotechnology: Principles and Processes: a dependency-led recombinant-DNA concept workflow, from defined DNA to recovery.

## Originality and differences

- Closest reviewed guides: Five Kingdom Classification, Plant Kingdom and Locomotion and Movement for Animal Kingdom; Cell Theory, Locomotion and Movement and Digestion and Absorption for Structural Organisation; Molecular Tools, Recombinant DNA Technology and Biotechnology Applications for Biotechnology Principles.
- Animal Kingdom uses an evidence grid and exclusion routine rather than a kingdom decision tree or plant trait staircase.
- Structural Organisation uses changes in composition across biological hierarchy and attachment-direction checks; it does not repeat the sarcomere sequence or a digestion route.
- Biotechnology Principles uses process dependencies and failure points, while the existing related guides are tool matching, a focused recombinant workflow and applications.
- An exact normalised eight-word scan against all other authored guide body, table and aside text found no remaining matches.
