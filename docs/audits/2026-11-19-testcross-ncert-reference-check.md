# Testcross guide: NCERT citation correction (2026-11-19)

## Finding

The guide cited NCERT Class XII Biology, Chapter 4, section 4.1 as “Inheritance of One Gene.” The official NCERT PDF identifies section 4.1 as “Mendel’s Laws of Inheritance” and section 4.2 as “Inheritance of One Gene.” The test-cross explanation appears in the Chapter 4 discussion of the monohybrid cross, on printed page 58, within the one-gene inheritance material. The site citation has therefore been corrected to section 4.2.

## Source verification

- Official NCERT Chapter 4 PDF: [Principles of Inheritance and Variation](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lebo104.pdf)
- PDF cover/reprint footer: Reprint 2026–27.
- PDF contents page: section 4.1 “Mendel’s Laws of Inheritance”; section 4.2 “Inheritance of One Gene.”
- PDF printed page 58: describes the test cross used to infer the genotype of a dominant-phenotype plant.
- The already-listed OpenStax Biology 2e “Test Cross” source remains as a second explanatory reference.

## Scope and limits

This check verifies the cited NCERT edition, section title, and the location of the test-cross discussion. It does not certify the guide’s full biology content, original diagram or interactive behavior, or count as human subject-matter review. Those items remain open in the content disposition record. No NCERT wording or figure was copied into the guide.

## Change made

Corrected the guide’s in-text NCERT anchor and source label from section 4.1 to section 4.2; identified the 2026–27 reprint. This is a citation correction, not a new teaching claim.
