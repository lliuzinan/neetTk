# Pedigree guide: NCERT section correction and source mapping (2026-11-23)

## Finding

The guide correctly cited NCERT section 4.8.1 for pedigree analysis but incorrectly pointed to section 4.1 for segregation. In the official current Class XII textbook, section 4.1 is “Mendel’s Laws of Inheritance”; the law of segregation is subsection 4.2.2 under “Inheritance of One Gene.” The anchor now names the correct section, edition, and checked printed page.

## Verified source

- Official NCERT PDF: [Principles of Inheritance and Variation](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lebo104.pdf)
- Reprint: 2026–27, as printed in the PDF.
- Printed p. 59: section 4.2.2, “Law of Segregation.”
- Printed p. 72: section 4.8.1, “Pedigree Analysis,” and Figure 4.13, standard pedigree symbols.

## Scope and limits

This verifies citation locations only; it does not review the guide’s complete inheritance logic, conditional probability example, X-linked route, health boundary, or MedQGo illustration/interactive. No reviewer is recorded for this guide. The site’s family, counterfactual and probability examples remain independently constructed material; no NCERT example or artwork was copied.

## Change

Corrected the in-text NCERT anchor from section 4.1 to section 4.2.2 and expanded the source label to identify sections 4.8.1 and 4.2.2 in the 2026–27 reprint. No teaching example, figure, or interactive changed.
