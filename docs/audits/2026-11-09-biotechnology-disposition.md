## November 9 figure and source follow-up

Both checked-in original images were visually compared. `recombinant-dna-workflow-v1.png` runs donor DNA → vector → recombinant DNA → host cell → “Selection”, but the final plate also labels one colony “Target product”. That label can suggest that a selection plate itself yields or proves the final product; the prose correctly separates selection, expression and downstream recovery. Do not carry that panel into the destination unchanged. `biotechnology-recombinant-dna-order-v1.webp` is the clearer construction sequence (isolate/cut/ligate/introduce), but ends at host introduction and does not illustrate selection or recovery. Its limited scope should remain explicit in the caption and surrounding text. This is a visual-editorial finding for an actual subject reviewer, not approval of either complete mechanism.

The official NCERT Chapter 9 URL was attempted through the web reader and directly from the workspace but timed out or failed TLS. An official ePathshala search result identified Chapter 9, yet did not provide the chapter PDF contents. At that point the exact current edition and section claims remained unverified. [OpenStax Biology 2e, 17.1](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/17-1-biotechnology) was opened as a supplementary factual check for cutting, ligation and molecular cloning; its text or illustrations were not reused. A mirror search result suggested a 2025–26 reprint, but was not treated as official version evidence.

Follow-up: [NCERT's own Class XII Biology prelims](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lebo1ps.pdf) identify the book as a 2025–26 reprint and list Chapter 9, “Biotechnology: Principles and Processes,” beginning at page 163. This establishes the official book-level version and chapter placement, not the text or figure claims inside the inaccessible Chapter 9 PDF. Keep chapter-level verification open. The [ePathshala textbook notice](https://epathshala.nic.in/wp-content/doc/book/btextbook/textbook.htm) also restricts republication; no textbook wording, figure or PDF has been reused in this work.
