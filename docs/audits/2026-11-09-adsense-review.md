## Findings

1. High priority: overlapping user tasks in recombinant-dna-technology and biotechnology-principles-and-processes. Both lead through cutting, ligation, vector, host entry, selection and product recovery; both tables map stages to jobs/errors. The latter has more explicit dependencies and scope. Assess merging the former into the latter, preserving useful distinctions and relevant redirects. This is qualitative overlap, not proof of copying or a reason to delete solely by word count.
2. High priority: factual/editorial maintenance unfinished. Live neuron routine says four steps then lists five elements. Mutation guide uses heritable without clarifying cell-lineage versus offspring and gives frameshift wording without explicit coding-region scope. The latter two require review, not automatic declarations of falsehood. Mendelian bounded clarification remains a draft. Better references alone do not establish article correctness.
3. High priority: actual manual review/curation evidence is incomplete. Team/editorial pages correctly avoid claiming all guides were reviewed. Honest disclosure helps but does not replace review. Google inventory-value guidance explicitly addresses automatically generated content without manual review or curation. Do not invent a requirement for a named professor or universal credentials.
4. Medium priority: substantial function improvements now exist (recording, backups, topic navigation, review dates, blank PDF), but the blank form is a supporting tool, not enough alone to establish subject-specific unique value. A checked filled example and integrated explanation/feedback journey would materially improve usefulness.
5. Medium priority: workbook page delivers an actual resource but still includes future-work/early-access sections. This is not automatically a violation; prioritize available resources, distinguish actual delivery from proposed additions and remove misleading promises if found.
6. Unknown: legitimate use/returning users. No numerical traffic threshold or mandatory waiting period was established by consulted policies. Verify with real feedback and authorized existing analytics; no artificial impressions or visits.
7. Requires separate check: root layout loads AdSense script globally. Script presence is not proof ads are served on any particular page. Review actual page-level eligibility and account exclusions before approval; no account settings changed here.
