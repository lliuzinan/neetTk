# Linkage guide: NCERT citation check (2026-11-21)

## Finding

The linkage guide named the NCERT subsection but omitted its number and edition. The official NCERT Class XII Biology Chapter 4 PDF, Reprint 2026–27, identifies “4.3.3 Linkage and Recombination” on printed page 67. The Morgan linkage examples are captioned as Figure 4.11 on printed page 68. The guide's in-text anchor and source label now give those details.

## Verified source

- Official NCERT PDF: [Principles of Inheritance and Variation](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lebo104.pdf)
- Reprint: 2026–27, as printed in the official PDF.
- Printed p. 67: subsection 4.3.3, “Linkage and Recombination.”
- Printed p. 68: Figure 4.11 caption describes two dihybrid crosses conducted by Morgan and notes the relative strength of linkage.
