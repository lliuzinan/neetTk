# Mendelian inheritance guide: NCERT citation check (2026-11-22)

## Finding

The guide's NCERT source link identified only the chapter, although its central lesson covers the single-gene inheritance model, dominance and segregation. The official NCERT Class XII Chapter 4 PDF names section 4.2 “Inheritance of One Gene” and identifies its relevant subsections on printed page 59. The visible source label now provides those locations and the current reprint year.

## Verified source

- Official NCERT PDF: [Principles of Inheritance and Variation](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lebo104.pdf)
- Edition: Reprint 2026–27, printed in the official PDF.
- Printed p. 55: section 4.2, “Inheritance of One Gene.”
- Printed p. 59: subsection 4.2.1, “Law of Dominance,” and subsection 4.2.2, “Law of Segregation.”

## Scope and limits

This is a source-location correction. The guide's presented prose/table version has an Owner-reported human review dated 2026-11-12, recorded as that version's review only. It does not establish expert credentials, site-wide review, or review of later unrelated material. The guide's asset provenance/label inspection was not separately assessed in this source check.
