# Inheritance learning outcomes — internal mapping

Checked 28 October 2026 against `lib/inheritanceLearning.ts` and the NMC NEET (UG) 2026 syllabus hosted by NTA: https://nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20260108180635.pdf , Biology Unit 7, PDF page 17 (printed syllabus page 14).

This is a route-level editorial map, not an article-by-article scientific approval or 2027 exam coverage claim. Textbook subsection mapping remains pending; see [source register](inheritance-source-rights-register.md). No official exercise is approved for publication by this document.

| Existing route / guide slug | Observable learning outcome | Syllabus relationship | Original evidence of learning to request | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Segregation / `mendelian-inheritance` | Separate parental genotype, gamete alleles and offspring phenotype. | Mendelian inheritance. | A learner-made allele-to-gamete explanation with assumptions stated. | A phenotype alone need not establish genotype. Avoid treating expected ratios as quotas. |
| Testcross / `testcross-and-unknown-genotypes` | Explain what a recessive tester reveals and what a small family cannot prove. | A teaching application of Mendelian inheritance; finite-sample inference is supporting reasoning, not a separately named syllabus heading. | A claim, evidence and uncertainty record using the learner's own working. | Do not equate an all-dominant sample with proof of homozygosity, or a likelihood with a posterior probability. |
| Expression / `incomplete-dominance-and-codominance` | Distinguish intermediate expression from distinguishable simultaneous expression. | Both expression patterns are explicitly included. | A comparison separating allele transmission from expression. | Do not imply that incomplete dominance blends or destroys alleles. |
| Two loci / `dihybrid-crosses-and-probability` | Decide whether independence justifies multiplying single-locus probabilities. | Supports Mendelian inheritance and the transition to linkage. | State the independence and survival assumptions before calculating. | A familiar ratio is not valid for every two-locus cross. |
| Linkage / `linkage-and-recombination-frequency` | Identify parental combinations from phase and calculate the scored recombinant proportion. | Linkage and crossing over are explicitly included. | Explain the numerator, denominator and limits of the observed fraction. | Offspring proportions are not percentages of meiotic cells with crossing over; avoid claiming precise long-distance maps from a simple proportion. |

## Scope gaps and sequencing

The five-step route is a selected inheritance foundation, not all of Unit 7. The syllabus also names blood groups/multiple alleles, pleiotropy, elementary polygenic inheritance, chromosome theory, sex determination and specified genetic disorders. Molecular genetics and evolution also belong to Unit 7 but are outside this route. Audit the existing related guides before adding any pages to cover these areas.

The next original worksheet should follow the five outcomes above with blank fields for **claim, evidence, assumption, calculation and limit**. It can ask readers to record the source/version of material they consulted without copying any source questions or answers. No new generated question bank is needed.
