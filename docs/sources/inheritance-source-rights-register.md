## What each future selected item must record

Owner; exact title and edition/reprint; exam date and language; booklet/paper code; question number; ordinary versus accessible alternative; official question URL; final/revised answer URL and revision date; independently checked reasoning; current syllabus reference; permitted intended use; permission evidence and restrictions; verification date; reviewer identity only if real review occurred.

An item is publishable only when these fields are resolved for the intended use. Blank fields mean not verified, not not-applicable. The final key may be traced without copying its full contents into MedQGo.
