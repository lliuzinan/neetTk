## Special modes of reproduction in flowering plants

- Slug: `special-modes-of-reproduction-in-flowering-plants`
- Learning outcome: distinguish apomixis, parthenocarpy and polyembryony by developmental structure, fertilisation relationship and embryo count.
- Closest guides reviewed: `sexual-reproduction-in-flowering-plants`, `seed-development-and-ploidy`, and `morphology-of-flowering-plants`.
- Difference: this guide focuses on reproductive exceptions and explicitly notes that apomixis and polyembryony can describe different axes of the same seed; it does not repeat double fertilisation or the full seed tissue/ploidy map.
- Original asset: `public/images/biology/special-modes-of-reproduction-v1.webp`.
- Sources: NTA/NMC NEET-UG 2026 Biology syllabus, Unit 6; NCERT Class 12 Biology Chapter 1 and its Biology Exemplar.

## Five flowering-plant families

- Slug: `five-plant-families-floral-identification`
- Learning outcome: identify the five families named in NEET-UG 2026 Unit 2 through distinct floral, inflorescence and fruit clues.
- Closest guides reviewed: `morphology-of-flowering-plants`, `anatomy-of-flowering-plants`, and `plant-kingdom`.
- Difference: this guide compares family-level diagnostic bundles and keeps inflorescence, floret and flower observation levels explicit; it does not repeat organ modifications or transverse-section anatomy.
- Original asset: `public/images/biology/five-plant-families-floral-identification-v1.webp`.
- Sources: NTA/NMC NEET-UG 2026 Biology syllabus, Unit 2; NCERT Class 11 Biology Chapter 5; Virginia Tech Extension botanical material; Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew overview of Asteraceae florets.
