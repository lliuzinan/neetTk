# Pollination identity-map brief

Date: 2026-10-14. English MedQGo NEET-UG Biology.

Learning outcome: classify a pollen transfer by flower and plant identity, then separately assess what floral organisation or compatibility permits.

NCERT anchor: Class 12 Biology Chapter 1, section 1.2.3 Pollination, including outbreeding devices and pollen-pistil interaction. Official PDF https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lebo101.pdf . Chapter text accessed via https://philoid.com/ncert/chapter/lebo101 ; the locally downloaded mirror PDF from October 13 is Reprint 2025-26. No claim of retrieving a newer reprint. Supplementary mechanism check: OpenStax Biology 2e 32.2, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/32-2-pollination-and-fertilization . Retain NCERT's three-way terminology.

Relationships: same flower vs same individual plant; route vs transport agent; monoecy vs dioecy; deposition vs compatibility vs fertilisation. Confusions: a bee can transfer self-pollen; geitonogamy crosses flowers but not individual plants; male/female flowers on one plant prevent autogamy but not geitonogamy; a changed flowering time within one flower does not automatically exclude pollen from another flower on that plant.

Closest guides inspected: sexual-reproduction-in-flowering-plants (reproductive route through fusion), pollen-development-and-male-gametophyte (cell lineage), special-modes-of-reproduction-in-flowering-plants (seed/fruit/embryo distinctions). New learning path uses identity boundaries and a transfer log rather than a cell-development route or developmental-exception list. Comparison table reads across three transfer classes; visual shows two separately rooted plants with letter-coded flower transfers; recall task changes plant ownership of a flower. All prose, examples and artwork original.

Hypothetical record: 40 transfer events, 12 within one flower, 18 between flowers of the same plant and 10 between individual plants. Interpret 30/45/25% as event classes only, not seed set or genetic diversity. Three published contextual links planned: pollen development prerequisite, full reproduction follow-on, Mendelian inheritance for selfing/segregation distinction.
